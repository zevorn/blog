### QEMU Boot Camp (2025) Introduction

QEMU Bootcamp (2025) is a public-interest foundational technical bootcamp hosted by the Gwei Open Source Community (GTOC) and co-organized by OpenCamp Bootcamp. This bootcamp is dedicated to helping developers understand core virtualization technologies and systematically master QEMU’s hardware modeling methods and the practical application of virtualization tools.

Through a course design and hands-on practice that progress from basic to advanced, participants will learn QEMU’s basic architecture, device emulation principles, peripheral modeling techniques, and the setup and debugging of system simulation environments. We focus not only on developing technical skills, but also on inspiring participants’ interest in low-level foundational software and deepening their understanding of computer architecture, operating systems, and the mechanisms by which hardware and software work together.

Whether you are a computer-related major student or an engineer looking to expand your system-level development skills, the 2025 QEMU Bootcamp will open the door to system virtualization and the open-source world.

By 8:00 PM on October 12, 2025, before the opening ceremony, a total of 858 participants had registered, from 379 universities, 231 companies, and 80 cities.
